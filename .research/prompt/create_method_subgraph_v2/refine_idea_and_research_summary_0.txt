# Novelty
Among the cited works, the closest baselines are (1) ‘Language Generation with Strictly Proper Scoring Rules’, which replaces log-likelihood with an alternative rule (Brier/Spherical) for the entire training run, and (2) DiscoPOP, which learns a new non-convex loss for preference (reward-model based) tuning.  None of them proposes 
• a time-varying, curriculum-style mixture that starts as pure cross-entropy and gradually shifts to a calibration-oriented score; 
• the joint scheduling of temperature scaling that softens logits in lock-step with the mixture weight; 
• a design that keeps the overall loss convex in the logits so that standard first-order fine-tuning pipelines remain unchanged.  
The only related idea is a fixed 50-50 CE+Brier mixture occasionally used as a baseline; the adaptive schedule and tied temperature are new, and they explicitly target the speed vs. calibration trade-off highlighted as an open issue in prior work.  Therefore, while the building blocks (CE, Brier, temperature scaling) are known, their synergistic, step-dependent integration for LLM token-level fine-tuning has not been reported in the supplied literature, giving the method moderate originality.

# Significance
Over-confidence, mis-calibration and consequent hallucination are recognised practical obstacles for deploying fine-tuned LLMs.  TAHLB addresses these issues while keeping the same computational footprint and code complexity as the default CE objective, unlike preference-optimisation or ZO methods that require extra models, prompts or costly queries.  The expected 30–50 % ECE reduction and improved robustness on Flores-200/jailbreak still matching CE task quality would, if confirmed, be of direct value to both academia (better evaluation of probabilistic outputs; research into trustworthy LLMs) and industry (safer user-facing systems without higher inference cost).  Nevertheless, the contribution is incremental—an improved loss function rather than a new training paradigm—and its impact depends on empirical reproducibility across more tasks and larger models.  Hence its significance is solid but not transformative.
